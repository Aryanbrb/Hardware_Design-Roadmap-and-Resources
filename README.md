# Hardware Design Roadmap and Resources

Hardware design is a whole subject in it's own, it's so vast that colleges should have it as a subject to cover in a semester or two. Anyways, anyone having interest in learning Hardware Design should not be restricted with the limiation of resources and guidance. Being a self-taught Hardware Design with a little to no guidance I understand, and even went through that hardship of finding right resources at right time. this article will try to mention each and every resource that I have went through to learn myself the art of Hardware Design. Now please mind that by Hardware Design some people also mean the IC design part, the digital and analog design. Unfortunately I am not that guy, my Hardware Design means PCB Hardware Design, something that involves designing computer hardware. If you are interested in learning IC Hardware Design please go [through this guide](https://github.com/abdelazeem201/ASIC-Design-Roadmap). There also this [link](https://github.com/m3y54m/Embedded-Engineering-Roadmap) that will help you learn the Embedded Systems, which can be a great refresher if you are a college student. This article includes a road-map as well so please feel free to use that for your reference, might not be the very accurate since the roadmap is hugely based on experience and every person experiences different paths but it will light you up through most of things. Now since learning is a ever-longing process, expect the resources to get updated in time being.
